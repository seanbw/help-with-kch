Why this space exists

Listening is part
of good care.

Care, Heard brings a personal blog and a community space together: one place to document my experiences of care and to make room for other people to tell their own stories.

The blog

One person's account, shared honestly.

The blog begins with my own experiences as a patient, including my concerns about treatment, communication and access to information. I want to describe what happened from my perspective and ask questions about the policies and practices that shape care.

These posts are personal accounts, not independent investigations, medical advice or a claim to represent every patient or member of staff. Where a story is ongoing or reflects my understanding, I say so.

The community

Different experiences deserve space.

Guests can register with a display name and share one contribution of up to 500 words — about one page. Stories should describe the writer's own experience, respect other people's privacy and avoid naming or identifying individuals.

The current version is a static prototype. It does not have real accounts, moderation tools or a shared server: a story submitted here is saved only in the visitor's browser and cannot be seen by other visitors.

A note to readers

Share with care.
Read with respect.

Health and care experiences can be deeply personal. Please protect your own privacy and the privacy of others. This site is not an emergency service and does not offer clinical or legal advice.
